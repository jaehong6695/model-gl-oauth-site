Privacy Policy

MODEL-GL Release Controller is an owner-operated, non-commercial software-development utility used for release management and verification.

Google data accessed

The application may access Google Apps Script project and deployment information and Google Sheets data required by the owner's automation.

Purpose of access

Google data is used only for owner-requested deployment, verification, release-management, and spreadsheet-backed automation operations.

Credential storage

OAuth credentials are stored only in protected local storage or encrypted repository secrets controlled by the owner. Secret values are not published here.

Sharing, sale, and advertising

Google user data is not sold, used for advertising, or shared for unrelated purposes. Infrastructure providers may process data only as required to execute the requested automation.

Retention and revocation

Operational data is retained only as needed for the owner's automation. The owner may revoke Google OAuth access at any time through Google Account security settings.
